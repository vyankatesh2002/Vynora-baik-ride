Common questions

FAQ.

Is it safe for solo women?
Absolutely. Zero-harassment policy, Aadhaar-verified rider, live location sharing available at any time, ISI helmet provided, first aid kit onboard.
Night rides allowed?
Yes — within Nashik city limits only, for your safety and ours. Well-lit routes preferred.
How does payment work?
Pay after your ride. UPI and cash both accepted. No advance payment required. No hidden charges, ever.
Is a helmet provided?
Yes. A clean, ISI-certified pillion helmet is provided for every single ride — always sanitised.
How do I book?
Message on WhatsApp with your pickup location, destination, and time. We confirm within 15 minutes.
Can I change my route mid-ride?
Yes, absolutely. Plans change — we're flexible. Just let us know and we adapt at no extra charge.
What if I need to cancel?
Free cancellation anytime before the ride starts. Just drop a message — no questions asked.
Which areas do you cover?
All Nashik city areas plus vineyard routes, Trimbakeshwar, and nearby spots. Ask for any custom location.
